7 Wonders of the World

The Seven Wonders of the World are famous monuments known for their beauty and history.

  1. Taj Mahal - India
  2. Great Wall of China - China
  3. Christ the Redeemer - Brazil
  4. Petra - Jordan
  5. Colosseum - Italy
  6. Machu Picchu - Peru
  7. Chichen Itza - Mexico
